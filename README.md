<h1>🎬 RAMP - Privacy Policy</h1>
<p class="updated">Last Updated: October 5, 2026</p>

<div class="box">
    <strong>Summary:</strong> RAMP does not collect, share, or transmit any personal data. All your data stays on your device.

<h2>1. Introduction</h2>
<p>RAMP ("we", "our", "the app") is a film production management application developed by Reelook. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle your information.</p>

<h2>2. Information We Collect</h2>
<p><strong>We do NOT collect any personal information.</strong></p>
<p>RAMP is a 100% offline application. We do not have servers, and no data is transmitted to us or any third party.</p>

<h2>3. Data Storage</h2>
<p>All data you enter into RAMP — including:</p>
<ul>
    <li>Company and project information</li>
    <li>Technician, artiste, and employee details</li>
    <li>Payment records and amounts</li>
    <li>Bill photographs</li>
    <li>Budget and expense information</li>
</ul>
<p>...is stored <strong>locally on your device only</strong> in an encrypted database.</p>

<h2>4. Permissions We Request</h2>
<ul>
    <li><strong>Camera:</strong> To capture bill photos (only when you choose to)</li>
    <li><strong>Storage:</strong> To save bill photos and backup files</li>
    <li><strong>Biometric:</strong> For app lock security (optional)</li>
</ul>
<p>These permissions are used only for their stated purpose. No data from these permissions is transmitted anywhere.</p>

<h2>5. Backup & Restore</h2>
<p>RAMP allows you to export backup files (.ramp). These files:</p>
<ul>
    <li>Are created and saved on your device</li>
    <li>Are shared only by YOU through your chosen method (WhatsApp, Drive, etc.)</li>
    <li>Contain only the data you have entered</li>
</ul>

<h2>6. Third-Party Services</h2>
<p>RAMP uses Google Play Billing for in-app purchases. When you make a purchase:</p>
<ul>
    <li>Payment is processed by Google Play</li>
    <li>We do not receive your payment information</li>
    <li>Please review Google's Privacy Policy for details</li>
</ul>
<p>No other third-party services are used.</p>

<h2>7. Data Security</h2>
<p>Your data is protected by:</p>
<ul>
    <li>Device-level encryption</li>
    <li>Optional App Lock (PIN + Biometric)</li>
    <li>No internet transmission = no interception risk</li>
</ul>

<h2>8. Children's Privacy</h2>
<p>RAMP is intended for business use by adults. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13.</p>

<h2>9. Your Rights</h2>
<p>You have complete control over your data:</p>
<ul>
    <li><strong>View:</strong> All data is visible within the app</li>
    <li><strong>Export:</strong> Backup feature exports all your data</li>
    <li><strong>Delete:</strong> Uninstalling the app deletes all data</li>
</ul>

<h2>10. Changes to This Policy</h2>
<p>We may update this policy. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.</p>
